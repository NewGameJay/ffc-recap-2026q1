A2. April attribution-gap backfill methodology

Full server-side Meta attribution wasn't operational until Jeff's CAPI rebuild went live April 28, 2026. Throughout the window, the existing pixel setup captured a partial subset of events. The data confirms this:

Pixel event type First captured Last captured Records
view_content 2026-02-13 2026-03-09 (then stopped) 59
add_to_cart 2026-02-18 2026-03-04 (then stopped) 42
purchase 2026-02-19 2026-04-28 (continued, low volume) 35
initiate_checkout 2026-02-19 2026-04-28 (continued) 97
complete_registration 2026-03-20 (cohort launch) 2026-04-28 93

April had 3× the click volume of March (4,597 vs. 1,537) but only 9 attributed purchases (vs. 47 in March) — a clear under-capture, regardless of whether the partial pixel was originally configured to handle the full Chargebee subscription flow or whether existing event paths stopped working mid-window. The April 28 CAPI rebuild is the first complete attribution path; the back-fill estimate accounts for the under-capture during the partial-coverage period.

Three methods to estimate the unattributed April revenue

Method Logic Estimated missing
Apply March's ROAS (4.18x) to April spend Floor — uses March's already-degraded performance $14,552.47
Apply Feb+Mar blended ROAS (7.03x) to April spend Middle — what April should have shown if the pixel held $28,515.01
Apply March's revenue-per-click ($10.07) to April clicks Click-volume direct estimate $40,298.62

The headline $28,515 uses the middle estimate. The conservative floor is $14,552; the aggressive ceiling is $40,299.

Source query:

SELECT
  DATE_TRUNC(date, MONTH) AS month, SUM(spend) AS spend,
  SUM(inline_link_clicks) AS clicks
FROM `...basic_ad`
WHERE date BETWEEN '2026-02-01' AND '2026-04-29'
GROUP BY 1;

-- April: $4,907.87 spend, 4,597 clicks
-- Feb+Mar blended ROAS = ($21,788 + $15,474) / ($1,603 + $3,698) = 7.03x
-- Estimated April revenue at blended ROAS = $4,907.87 × 7.03 = $34,497.41
-- April captured = $5,982.40
-- Missing = $28,515.01

Email program — depth view

Within the $37,506 email-attributed deals: 23 of those subscribers can be tied directly to a click event on one of MH-1's 22 published marketing emails in the window — $1,679.70 in MRR / $20,156.40 annualized recurring revenue.

Source query:

WITH classified AS (
  SELECT
    SAFE_CAST(property_amount AS FLOAT64) AS amount,
    CASE WHEN property_hs_analytics_source IN ('PAID_SOCIAL','EMAIL_MARKETING','SOCIAL_MEDIA','ORGANIC_SEARCH','OTHER_CAMPAIGNS','REFERRALS')
      OR property_hs_analytics_latest_source IN ('PAID_SOCIAL','EMAIL_MARKETING','SOCIAL_MEDIA','ORGANIC_SEARCH','OTHER_CAMPAIGNS','REFERRALS')
      THEN 'attributed' ELSE 'other' END AS bucket
  FROM `...hubspot.deal`
  WHERE property_hs_is_closed_won = TRUE
    AND DATE(property_hs_closed_won_date) BETWEEN '2026-02-01' AND '2026-04-29'
)
SELECT bucket, COUNT(*) AS deals, ROUND(SUM(amount),2) AS revenue
FROM classified GROUP BY 1;
-- attributed: 139 deals, $105,526.20
-- (subtract $1,877 paid_social already in Meta) → $103,649 net
