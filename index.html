How the attribution works (in plain English)

HubSpot tags every paid customer with two attribution data points:

  • First touch answers "how did we acquire this person?" — the marketing channel that first brought them to the site.
  • Last touch answers "which channel closed them?" — the channel they used right before converting.

A deal counts in this report as MH-attributable if either the first touch OR last touch is a marketing channel MH-1 operates. Each deal counts once (no double-counting within HubSpot data).

On revenue framing: - Cash collected = actual money in the bank from purchases in this window (one-time amounts, including upfront yearly subscription payments). - ARR (annualized recurring revenue) = the monthly subscription rate × 12, projecting what the customer pays over a full year. ARR is a forward indicator.

Meta-attributed revenue ($43,244) uses Meta's own platform attribution (7-day click / 1-day view, captured server-side via the pixel). It's largely complementary to HubSpot — Meta tracked ticket purchases that ran through a different flow than the membership deals HubSpot tracks.
